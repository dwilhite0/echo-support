FIRM is a law firm management application designed for solo attorneys and small law firms to organize the information they use every day.

FIRM helps authorized firm members manage clients, cases, court dates, payments and balances, tasks and reminders, professional contacts, and other day-to-day firm information in one shared workspace.

Firm Access & iCloud

FIRM uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit to synchronize shared firm information between authorized members.

Each user should be signed into their Apple/iCloud account on their device and have iCloud available for FIRM.

Synchronization depends on internet connectivity and Apple service availability. Changes made by another firm member may not appear instantly on a device that is offline or has not yet synchronized.

Tasks & Notifications

FIRM can provide reminders for tasks, court dates, and other supported events.

Notification permissions can be reviewed in the iPhone Settings app. Some reminders depend on information first synchronizing to the device.

Subscriptions

FIRM subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple. Subscription tiers provide the same core FIRM functionality and differ by the number of authorized firm members supported.

Subscription purchases, renewals, cancellations, and payment credentials are handled through Apple's App Store and subscription management system.

If a subscription purchase is not appearing correctly in FIRM, use the available Restore Purchases option before contacting support.

Account & Firm Deletion

Available account and firm deletion controls are located inside FIRM.

Removing a staff member from a firm is different from deleting the firm's shared workspace.

Firm deletion is destructive. Before permanently deleting a firm, the Firm Owner should preserve any information the firm is required to retain outside FIRM.

Deleting FIRM data does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store subscription. Apple subscriptions are managed separately through Apple's subscription settings.
