FIRM Privacy Policy

E.C.H.O. — Even Chaos Has Order | Arkansas, United States

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FIRM uses and protects information when law firms use the FIRM application. FIRM is a native Apple application that stores working data on the user's device and uses Apple iCloud/CloudKit to synchronize and share authorized firm records between approved members of the same firm.

Who Operates FIRM

E.C.H.O. Even Chaos Has Order ("E.C.H.O.," "we," "us," or "our"), based in Arkansas, United States, operates FIRM.

Privacy and support contact: evenchaoshasorder@gmail.com

Each firm decides what information to enter into FIRM and which people are authorized to participate in its shared workspace. Questions about the substance of a client's legal matter should ordinarily be directed to the responsible law firm.

Information FIRM May Process

FIRM processes information users choose to enter or that is needed to operate the app. Depending on how a firm uses FIRM, this may include:

  • User and firm information, such as a user's name, contact information, firm affiliation, role, access status, and Apple/iCloud-based identity information used to authorize shared access.
  • Client and case information, such as names, aliases, phone numbers, dates of birth, case numbers, charges, courts, court dates and history, judges, prosecutors, outcomes, notes, and related case details.
  • Optional sensitive intake information, when a firm chooses to enter it, such as Social Security numbers, driver's-license numbers, sex, race, custody information, arrest information, probation or parole information, approved contacts, and related identifiers or history.
  • Financial record information entered by the firm, such as quoted fees, payments recorded as received, payment dates and notes, paid-to-date totals, and balances. FIRM does not process clients' credit-card or bank-account credentials through its client payment-entry workflow.
  • Tasks, reminders, court follow-up information, completion history, and activity attribution.
  • Professional contact/Rolodex information entered by the firm.
  • Subscription and entitlement information supplied through Apple StoreKit that is needed to determine subscription status and firm member capacity.
  • Support communications a user chooses to send to E.C.H.O.

How Information Is Used

FIRM uses information only as needed to provide and maintain the app's functionality, including to:

  • Create and display the firm workspace and its records.
  • Allow authorized firm members to access and collaborate on shared firm information.
  • Synchronize records through Apple iCloud/CloudKit.
  • Search and organize clients, cases, court information, tasks, contacts, and other records.
  • Calculate and display balances and recorded collection totals based on information entered by the firm.
  • Schedule local reminders and notifications on supported Apple devices.
  • Manage firm membership, permissions, and Quick Unlock reset status.
  • Verify Apple subscription entitlements and applicable staff capacity.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests and protect the security and integrity of the service.

Local Storage and Apple iCloud/CloudKit

FIRM uses persistent local storage on the user's Apple device so previously synchronized information can remain available after the app is closed and reopened. FIRM uses Apple iCloud/CloudKit private and shared database services to synchronize firm records between authorized members.

FIRM does not operate an E.C.H.O.-hosted central database containing customers' client and case records, and it does not use CloudKit's public database for confidential firm records.

CloudKit storage, synchronization, account availability, infrastructure recovery copies, and related platform operations are provided and controlled by Apple and are subject to Apple's services and policies.

Synchronization is not guaranteed to be instantaneous. A device that is offline may continue to contain previously synchronized local information until it reconnects and receives updated access or deletion information.

Shared Firm Access

FIRM is designed as a shared workspace. The firm Owner may invite authorized members through Apple's CloudKit sharing system. Administrators may manage staff access as permitted by the app and Apple's sharing permissions. Staff receive ordinary authorized workspace access according to their role.

Authorized members of the same firm may be able to view information entered into that firm's shared workspace, including sensitive client information the firm chooses to enter.

A user should enter sensitive information only when the firm is authorized to use it and it is reasonably necessary for the firm's work.

Removing a member from a CloudKit share prevents continuing cloud access after the change is received. FIRM cannot guarantee immediate remote erasure of information already downloaded to a device that remains offline.

Quick Unlock and Device Security

Quick Unlock is an optional device-local convenience feature. Its PIN verifier and retry information are stored using Apple Keychain and are not uploaded as the user's CloudKit identity credential.

Quick Unlock does not replace Apple/iCloud authorization for access to shared firm data. An authorized Administrator may invalidate a staff member's existing Quick Unlock access, but cannot view or recover that user's PIN.

Subscriptions and Payments

Apple processes App Store subscription purchases and payment credentials. FIRM uses StoreKit subscription and entitlement information needed to determine whether a FIRM subscription is active and what firm-member capacity applies.

E.C.H.O. does not receive a user's full App Store payment-card credentials.

Client payments recorded inside FIRM are administrative records entered by the firm. FIRM does not itself charge a law firm's clients or process their bank or payment-card transactions.

Notifications

FIRM uses Apple notification features for tasks, court reminders, follow-up prompts, and other enabled alerts.

After shared information reaches a device, FIRM may schedule local notifications on that device. Apple controls background delivery and synchronization timing, so a newly created item from another firm member may not appear instantly on a closed or offline device.

Notification content may appear on a device lock screen depending on the user's Apple notification settings. Users can manage notification permissions in device settings and available FIRM preferences.

Photos, Files, and User-Entered Content

Where the shipping version of FIRM allows a user to select or attach supported content, that content is used for the app functionality requested by the user and may be associated with the relevant firm record.

FIRM does not automatically import a user's address book, photo library, microphone recordings, or precise location.

Advertising, Tracking, Analytics, and AI

FIRM does not use third-party advertising networks, does not sell personal information, and does not use firm data for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

The shipping app does not use firm or client records for AI case analysis. FIRM's declared App Store data uses are for app functionality rather than advertising or tracking.

Account Deletion and Firm Deletion

Users can initiate applicable account or access deletion actions inside FIRM. For a non-Owner member, deleting the user's FIRM access removes that member's current access and local workspace data from that device, while shared firm records and appropriate historical attribution may remain with the firm.

Only the active Firm Owner can initiate Delete Firm & Account. That action is intended to permanently close the firm's active CloudKit workspace and remove its active firm records, including clients, cases, court history, payments, tasks, contacts, and memberships.

Other members then lose access to that shared workspace.

Deletion from the active FIRM workspace does not mean Apple infrastructure recovery copies are erased instantaneously. Apple controls CloudKit infrastructure, recovery, and backup lifecycle.

Users should preserve any records they are professionally or legally required to retain before confirming destructive deletion.

Deleting FIRM data does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Apple's supported subscription-management system.

Archiving and Historical Records

Archiving is different from deletion. Archiving a client or case preserves its history. Archiving a balance removes it from active receivable totals without erasing the underlying historical record.

Historical attribution may remain where needed to preserve the integrity of the firm's records even after a staff member no longer has active access.

Data Retention

FIRM retains active firm records in local storage and the applicable Apple CloudKit workspace for as long as they remain part of the firm's active or archived records, unless the firm deletes them through available functionality.

Device-local copies may remain until the app receives synchronization or access changes. Apple may retain infrastructure or recovery copies according to Apple's own service operations and policies.

Support correspondence sent to evenchaoshasorder@gmail.com may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support history, protect against misuse, or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Users may leave optional intake fields blank, edit information through available app controls, manage notification permissions, and use in-app deletion controls where available.

For questions about access, correction, deletion, or FIRM's handling of personal information, contact the responsible firm or E.C.H.O. at evenchaoshasorder@gmail.com .

A person whose information was entered by a law firm does not need a FIRM account to raise a privacy concern. Applicable rights and lawful retention obligations may depend on the circumstances and governing law.

Children

FIRM is a professional law-firm management application and is not directed to children. It is intended for attorneys and authorized law-firm personnel.

United States Availability

FIRM 1.0 is offered for App Store distribution in the United States. E.C.H.O. is based in Arkansas, United States. Apple's infrastructure may process data in locations determined by Apple's services.
