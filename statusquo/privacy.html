Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Your App Store Connect account stays yours. StatusQuo reads information directly from Apple using credentials you choose to import on your device.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the StatusQuo app, its widgets and Apple Watch companion, and these support pages. The app is provided by E.C.H.O. Even Chaos Has Order.

Your credentials and connection

You provide your own API Key ID and .p8 private key, with an Issuer ID and Vendor Number when your key type or reports require them. StatusQuo stores saved connection credentials in Apple’s Keychain with device-only, non-synchronizing protection and access while the device is unlocked. Credentials are not bundled with the app, sent to our server, or shared with another developer. Each device needs its own connection setup.

Authentication tokens are signed locally. Requests go over HTTPS to Apple’s App Store Connect API and Apple-provided report download endpoints. Apple app icons are downloaded from Apple’s image services. The private key is used locally to sign tokens; it is not uploaded to Apple as part of API requests. Apple receives the authenticated requests and ordinary connection information needed to serve them.

Information displayed and stored locally

Depending on permissions and Apple’s availability, StatusQuo processes app names and icons, versions, review/distribution states, TestFlight information, written customer reviews and reviewer display names, pricing, and sales, download, subscription and other reporting data. This is your connected account’s information, not information from the developer’s account.

Parsed reports, locally observed status changes, notification baselines and display summaries may be cached on your device. Observed changes are identified as observations, not a retrospective history supplied by Apple. Saved report and display files are excluded from backup where implemented. The original .p8 files you selected remain subject to the storage service where you keep them; importing a file does not delete that original.

Widgets, Watch and notifications

Widgets use a local shared display cache. A paired Apple Watch can receive display summaries through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service; private keys and authentication tokens are not included. Information on a Home Screen, Lock Screen, Watch or notification may be visible to people who can see that screen. You control widget placement, notification permissions and notification previews in Apple’s settings.

Notifications are optional and generated locally from observed changes. Background refresh depends on iOS scheduling, connectivity and protected-data availability. StatusQuo does not operate a push notification server for this feature.

Purchases

Apple handles subscription purchases and payment information. StatusQuo uses StoreKit to load current products and prices and verify subscription access, expiration or revocation. We do not receive your card details. Restoring Pro on another eligible device through Apple does not transfer your App Store Connect private keys.

Tracking and third parties

The current app contains no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDK. It does not send your account reports or keys to a developer-operated server. Apple processes API, purchase and platform diagnostic information under its own policies. Diagnostic sharing through Apple is controlled by your Apple settings and services.

These static pages use no analytics scripts, embedded trackers, external fonts or contact forms. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Following external links is subject to the destination’s policies.

Your choices, retention and removal

Use “Disconnect & Remove Saved Keys” in StatusQuo to remove its saved connection credentials. Disconnect also stops its scheduled checks and clears its current widget display, but it does not erase every previously cached report or observed-history file. A paired Watch may need to reconnect to receive a cleared display. Disconnecting does not cancel an App Store subscription or revoke the API key at Apple.

Remove widgets and notification previews if you do not want information visible there. To remove local app-container data, delete the app rather than offloading it; remove the Watch app separately if needed. Apple may retain Keychain items across app deletion, so disconnect before deleting. Revoke an API key directly in App Store Connect if it should no longer authorize requests. Files in Files/iCloud Drive or other storage remain until you remove them there.

Support correspondence and changes

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it to address your request and related follow-up. Do not include private keys or tokens. This policy will be updated if the app’s data handling changes; the published page will show its revision date.
