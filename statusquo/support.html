Support

Last updated October 17, 2026

Help with connecting your account, understanding reports and managing StatusQuo Pro.

Contact support

Contact evenchaoshasorder@gmail.com for support and privacy questions. Do not send private keys, authentication tokens, passwords or unredacted financial reports.

Include your StatusQuo version, iOS version, the affected page and the exact error text. If useful, attach a screenshot after hiding Key IDs, Issuer IDs, Vendor Numbers, financial details and customer information. We will never need your .p8 private key, password or JWT token. Do not post private reports in public GitHub issues.

Connect your own account

In the connection screen, choose the appropriate Team or Individual key type, enter the Key ID and import its matching .p8 file. Team keys require the corresponding Issuer ID. Sales/report access may require a separate key and Vendor Number. Use the roles and access your organization authorizes. The private key remains in this device’s Keychain after saving; set up other devices separately.

Connection errors

  • Offline or timeout: verify connectivity, try another trusted network, then refresh. A timeout does not establish that a key is invalid.
  • 401/authentication: check the Key ID, key type, matching .p8, required Issuer ID, device clock and whether the key was revoked.
  • 403/permission or scope: note the specific failed endpoint and Apple error code. Have your account administrator check that key’s permissions; do not share its private key with support.
  • 404/report unavailable: Apple may not have generated that report, including dates without activity. StatusQuo must not substitute a zero.

Analytics, dates and deletions

Check the selected app, reporting period, currency, coverage dates and last-updated information before comparing totals. Apple’s Analytics dashboard, Sales and Trends and financial payment reports have different definitions and timing. Missing reports leave gaps; reported zeros remain zeros. Cached information stays visible while a fresh read runs.

Installations and Deletions require Apple to have generated the relevant Analytics Reports for your account. An account Admin may need to request ongoing report generation through Apple’s API outside StatusQuo. StatusQuo only reads existing reports and never creates report requests. Privacy thresholds, opted-in users, permissions and Apple’s delivery schedule may limit these reports.

Pro and Restore Purchases

Open the Pro screen to see Apple’s current plans and any eligible trial. If products are unavailable, do not assume a purchase completed. After a purchase, use the same Apple Account on your other supported devices and choose “Restore Purchases.” A pending Apple approval or payment must finish before access is granted. If access is still missing, contact support with the error text, never your API key.

Use “Manage Subscription” or Apple’s subscription management to review or cancel. For billing or a refund, use Apple’s purchase support. Basic status tracking and basic status widgets do not require Pro.

Widgets, Watch and notifications

Open StatusQuo to refresh its connection and latest display data, then check the widget’s timestamp. Keep the paired Watch connected so a new snapshot can transfer. Expanded widgets and Watch features require eligible Pro access. Allow notifications in iPhone Settings if desired. iOS decides when background refresh runs; the device’s protected-data state can postpone checks. Notifications describe changes observed by StatusQuo, not guaranteed instant Apple events.

Disconnect safely

Use “Disconnect & Remove Saved Keys” before removing the app or handing over a device. This removes saved credentials; it does not cancel your subscription, revoke the key at Apple, or erase every cached report. See the Privacy Policy for retention and removal details. Manage API key revocation directly in App Store Connect.
