Your authorized account

Connect only an App Store Connect account you are authorized to access, using your own credentials and the permissions your organization permits. Protect your private keys and comply with Apple’s agreements. StatusQuo is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.

Strictly read-only

StatusQuo retrieves information. It does not submit or release apps, edit metadata or pricing, manage testers, delete App Store Connect data, or reply to reviews. Account administration and report-generation setup must be performed outside StatusQuo by an authorized person. Buying StatusQuo Pro changes access inside StatusQuo, not your connected developer account.

Free and Pro

Basic app-review/status tracking, available status history, basic status notifications and basic Home Screen and Lock Screen status widgets remain free. Pro adds TestFlight, customer reviews, analytics and pricing, combined app analytics, expanded widgets, Watch features and expanded notifications, subject to Apple’s available data and permissions.

Pro is intended to offer monthly and annual auto-renewing subscription options. The App Store purchase sheet and StoreKit information shown in the app identify the actual available plans, local price, billing period and any eligible trial. This page does not promise a particular price or trial. No lifetime Pro purchase is offered by this model. Lifetime purchase prices shown in Analytics describe products belonging to your connected apps, not a StatusQuo plan.

Billing, trials and cancellation

Payment is handled by Apple using your Apple Account. An auto-renewing subscription continues until cancelled. If an eligible free trial is offered, its duration, renewal price and conditions are shown before purchase; it converts to the paid plan unless cancelled under Apple’s displayed terms. Manage or cancel through “Manage Subscription” in the app or your Apple Account subscriptions settings. Deleting or disconnecting StatusQuo does not cancel a subscription. Access continues for the period Apple reports as entitled, subject to refunds, revocations and applicable law.

Use “Restore Purchases” with the Apple Account used to subscribe to restore eligible access on supported devices. App Store Connect credentials are configured separately on each device. Refund requests and billing disputes are handled through Apple; see Apple’s purchase support.

Reporting limits

Information can be delayed, partial, cached, unavailable or restricted by Apple. A missing report is not a reported zero. StatusQuo observations are not Apple-provided historical events. Sales/proceeds displays reflect the available source reports and their reporting dates; estimated proceeds are not payment confirmation or financial advice. Review Apple’s original reports for financial, release or operational decisions.

Widgets and Watch show the latest successfully transferred display data and may lag behind the app. Background updates and notifications depend on Apple’s scheduling, device state, permissions and connectivity; immediate delivery is not guaranteed. Pro does not grant additional App Store Connect permissions or make unavailable reports available.
