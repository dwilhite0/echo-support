Getting Started with FIRM

User Guide | E.C.H.O. — Even Chaos Has Order

FIRM is a streamlined law firm management application designed for solo attorneys and small law firms. It brings commonly used firm information together in one organized workspace without unnecessary complexity.

Before You Begin

FIRM uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit to synchronize shared firm information between authorized members. Make sure the device is signed into the appropriate Apple/iCloud account and that iCloud is available.

1. Setting Up Your Firm

When FIRM is opened for the first time, follow the on-screen setup process to create or access your firm workspace.

The person responsible for the firm workspace serves as the Firm Owner. The Owner controls the firm and can manage authorized staff access.

2. Inviting Firm Members

FIRM allows authorized members of the same firm to work with shared firm information.

The Firm Owner can invite authorized staff through FIRM's supported Apple iCloud/CloudKit sharing process. Each participating user should use their own Apple/iCloud account on their device.

Access should only be provided to people authorized to view the firm's information.

3. Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a quick overview of important firm information. Depending on the firm's records, it may display items such as:

  • Calendar information
  • Upcoming court activity
  • Tasks and reminders
  • Outstanding balances
  • Current collection totals
  • Appointments or meetings
  • Professional contacts

Dashboard information is based on records entered into FIRM by authorized firm members.

4. Clients

Use the Clients section to create and manage client records.

Client records may contain contact information, case details, financial records, court information, notes, and other information the firm chooses to maintain.

Only enter sensitive client information when the firm is authorized to maintain it and it is reasonably necessary for the firm's work.

5. Cases

Case information can be associated with the appropriate client. FIRM can be used to organize information such as:

  • Case or cause numbers
  • Charges
  • Courts
  • Judges and prosecutors
  • Court dates
  • Case notes
  • Case history and outcomes

6. Court Dates

Court information entered into FIRM can be viewed through the application's calendar and court-related views.

Add or update court information when dates change so the firm's shared records remain current.

7. Payments and Balances

FIRM can be used to record financial information associated with a client or matter, including quoted fees, payments recorded as received, payment dates, notes, paid-to-date totals, and outstanding balances.

These entries are firm records. FIRM does not itself charge the firm's clients or process their bank or payment-card transactions.

8. Tasks and Reminders

Tasks can be used to keep track of work that needs attention. Authorized users can create, review, and complete tasks as supported by the app.

FIRM may also provide notifications for supported tasks, court reminders, follow-up items, and other enabled alerts.

Notification delivery depends on the device's notification settings, internet connectivity, synchronization, and Apple services.

9. Professional Contacts

FIRM includes a professional contact area for information the firm uses regularly, such as court officials and other professional contacts.

10. Search

Use FIRM's available search tools to locate supported records more quickly without manually browsing through each section.

11. Quick Unlock

Quick Unlock is an optional convenience feature for supported devices.

Quick Unlock does not replace the user's Apple/iCloud authorization for access to shared firm information.

If staff Quick Unlock access needs to be reset, an authorized Administrator may invalidate the existing Quick Unlock access. The Administrator cannot view or recover another user's PIN.

12. Synchronization

FIRM uses Apple iCloud/CloudKit to synchronize shared firm records between authorized members.

Synchronization is not always instantaneous. A device that is offline or has not yet synchronized may temporarily display older information.

If information does not appear immediately, confirm that the device has an internet connection and that the appropriate Apple/iCloud account is available.

13. Subscriptions

FIRM subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store system.

Subscription tiers provide the same core FIRM functionality and differ by the number of authorized firm members supported.

If an existing subscription is not appearing correctly, use the available Restore Purchases option.

14. Removing a Staff Member

When a staff member should no longer have access to the firm's shared workspace, an authorized Owner or Administrator should remove that member using the available staff-management controls.

Removing a member prevents continuing cloud access after the access change is received by Apple CloudKit. A device that remains offline may not receive the change immediately.

15. Account and Firm Deletion

FIRM provides applicable account and access deletion controls inside the application.

Removing an individual staff member is different from deleting the entire firm workspace.

Only the active Firm Owner can initiate the available Delete Firm & Account action.

Firm deletion is destructive. Before permanently deleting a firm, preserve any records the firm is professionally or legally required to retain.

Deleting FIRM data does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store subscription. Subscription management and cancellation are handled separately through Apple.
